What the evidence supports

EPA lead-safety guidance, FEMA fire-safety guidance and CFPB tenant-screening guidance ground recurring records, disclosures and escalation boundaries. They do not establish an automation rate, adoption prevalence or savings; owner decisions and safety response remain accountable human work.

Processes to test

Tenant maintenance request intake, deduplication and routing

Capture tenant-provided details, attachments and timestamps; detect duplicates and route by owner-configured categories. Do not diagnose hazards, deny habitability, authorize unsafe work or replace emergency response.

Measure
Net human minutes per correctly routed request including review, duplicate handling and exception escalation; missed-critical-request rate
Reject the idea if
Reject if critical or private requests are misrouted, emergency escalation is delayed, review/rework offsets time saved, or tenant data crosses an unauthorized boundary.
Human authority
Property manager or designated maintenance/safety owner determines urgency, vendor authorization and emergency response; tenant communications require authorized recipient and content review.

Unit inspection, repair evidence and turnover checklist review

Organize dated photos, checklists, readings and open items; flag missing evidence and compare prior records. Do not certify habitability, infer causation, set deposits or make a safety determination.

Measure
Review minutes per accepted turnover packet; missing-evidence, repeat-visit and critical-defect escalation rates
Reject the idea if
Reject if incomplete evidence is accepted as complete, lead or other safety concerns are suppressed, rework is not reduced, or private tenant records are exposed.
Human authority
Authorized property manager and qualified inspectors/contractors determine condition, remediation and lawful deposit/lease actions; safety specialists retain technical authority.

Tenant and vendor status follow-up

Draft status requests from current work-order state, send approved reminders to verified recipients and record replies. Do not promise repairs, alter lease terms, disclose another tenant's data or send emergency instructions without human control.

Measure
Net human minutes per accurate approved follow-up; response latency, duplicate-send and privacy-error rate
Reject the idea if
Reject if stale status, wrong-recipient disclosure or duplicate sends increase, required escalation is missed, or review/rework eliminates effort reduction.
Human authority
Property manager approves recipient, content, commitments and escalation; vendor and owner decisions remain with designated humans.

[buildium] [appfolio] [repo-property-builder]

Rental-application and tenant-screening document completeness review

Check whether required fields, consent and source documents are present, segregate access and surface contradictions for review. Do not make or recommend an eligibility, adverse-action, rent, lease or fair-housing decision.

Measure
Review minutes per complete application; missing-document, false-flag, correction and unauthorized-access rates
Reject the idea if
Reject if a missing field becomes an adverse decision, protected/private data is exposed, false flags or rework rise, or the system is treated as the lawful decision-maker.
Human authority
Owner or authorized housing professional makes lawful application and adverse-action decisions and controls notices; privacy/access owner controls retention and disclosure.

Business value and a falsifiable pilot

No manager baseline, achieved automation rate, review/rework data, operating cost, displacement proof or eligible-population measurement. Null is not zero.

Within one authorized manager, compare matched redacted or synthetic requests, inspections, follow-ups and applications under the incumbent and supervised candidate workflows; pre-register privacy, safety and owner-approval gates.

  • Human time including review/rework is not lower than matched baseline.
  • Any tenant record crosses an unauthorized boundary or an unapproved message, decision or safety action occurs.
  • A workflow is treated as a habitability, emergency, screening, lease or owner decision without authorized human review.
  • Measured first-year net cash is not positive for a cash-savings offer; capacity gains alone are not cash.
Read the shared value model

Limits

  • This is not housing, legal, safety, fair-housing, screening or property-management advice, product admission or an industry-wide forecast.
  • Current software adoption prevalence and subscription prices were not verified; named offerings are not assumed installed.
  • No generic repository is claimed to replace an owner's property system, inspection records, tenant privacy controls or professional authority.
  • Rights, security, retention, restoration, integrations, accessibility, emergency response and state/local requirements remain product-owner gates.
