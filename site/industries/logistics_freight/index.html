Processes to test

Customer load-intake and shipment-record completeness review

Capture requested pickup, delivery, commodity, equipment and contact fields; flag missing or contradictory fields for review. Do not quote binding terms, classify regulated goods or accept a shipment without authorized review.

Measure
Net human minutes per accepted complete load record including review, rework and exception handling; missed-critical-field rate
Reject the idea if
Reject if unsafe or regulated details are silently accepted, correction/rework rises, critical fields are missed or private data crosses an unauthorized boundary.
Human authority
Authorized operations staff determine acceptance, terms, commodity treatment and customer commitments; qualified safety and compliance personnel retain authority.

[descartes-tms] [repo-fleetbase]

Dispatch packet preparation and driver/carrier confirmation

Assemble approved load details, contact records and timestamps; draft confirmation requests and surface missing acknowledgements. Do not select carriers, assign unsafe work, alter hours-of-service records or issue binding instructions without human approval.

Measure
Net human minutes per accurately acknowledged dispatch packet including review and rework; missing-acknowledgement and unsafe-instruction rate
Reject the idea if
Reject if an acknowledgement is mistaken for legal or safety clearance, HOS records are changed or obscured, rework offsets time saved, or an unapproved instruction is sent.
Human authority
Dispatcher and designated carrier/safety authority approve assignment, instructions, records and escalations; the system is not a carrier-authority substitute.

Shipment-status exception detection and approved follow-up

Normalize status events, flag stale or contradictory milestones and draft follow-ups to verified recipients. Do not infer fault, promise delivery, change routing or send customer claims responses without human review.

Measure
Net human minutes per accurately triaged and approved exception; stale-event, duplicate-send, missed-escalation and privacy-error rates
Reject the idea if
Reject if stale or incorrect status causes a customer commitment, routing or claims error, review/rework is not reduced, or an escalation is missed.
Human authority
Operations lead approves status interpretation, customer commitments, routing changes and claims/escalations; verified recipients and channel controls remain mandatory.

[onfleet-platform] [descartes-tms] [repo-fleetbase]

Proof-of-delivery and freight-billing evidence reconciliation

Match approved delivery events, signed or electronic proof, accessorial evidence and invoice fields; surface mismatches for review. Do not approve payment, waive claims, alter invoices or determine contractual liability.

Measure
Net human minutes per correctly reconciled invoice packet including exception review; false-match, missed-mismatch and duplicate-payment rates
Reject the idea if
Reject if a mismatch is silently accepted, payment or claim authority is bypassed, false matches/rework rise, or evidence is unavailable for audit.
Human authority
Authorized finance and operations staff approve invoices, accessorials, claims and contractual interpretations; the workflow only organizes evidence.

Business value and a falsifiable pilot

No firm baseline, achieved automation rate, review/rework data, operating cost, displacement proof or eligible-population measurement. Null is not zero.

Within one authorized freight operator, compare matched redacted or synthetic loads, dispatches, exceptions and invoice packets under the incumbent and supervised candidate workflows; pre-register HOS, privacy, approval and rollback gates.

  • Human time including review/rework is not lower than matched baseline.
  • Any HOS, carrier-authority, routing, payment or customer commitment action occurs without authorized human approval.
  • Private shipment, driver or customer data crosses an unauthorized boundary or a critical exception is missed.
  • Measured first-year net cash is not positive for a cash-savings offer; capacity gains alone are not cash.

Limits

  • This is not transportation, safety, customs, legal, regulatory or carrier-authority advice, product admission or an industry-wide forecast.
  • Current software adoption prevalence and subscription prices were not verified; named offerings are not assumed installed.
  • No generic repository is claimed to replace a TMS, WMS, carrier records, HOS records, contractual authority or professional judgment.
