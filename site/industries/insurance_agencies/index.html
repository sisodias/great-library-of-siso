US independent insurance agencies

Start with supervised submission intake, document completeness, certificate/service requests and renewal preparation. Existing agency-management products already advertise broad workflow capabilities, so any pilot must compare against the actual incumbent and preserve carrier and licensed-producer authority.

What the evidence supports

BLS describes insurance-sales-agent work that includes interviewing applicants, explaining policies and contacting existing policyholders. That task inventory supports measuring administrative assistance around collection, reminders and document preparation; it does not establish an automation rate, carrier acceptance or permission to make coverage decisions.

Processes to test

New-business submission intake and completeness chase

Collect supplied applicant and risk fields, normalize documents and flag missing or inconsistent items; do not recommend coverage, bind or represent carrier acceptance.

Measure
Human minutes per complete, producer-accepted submission, including review, rework and exceptions
Reject the idea if
Reject if missing or conflicting risk facts are silently accepted, unauthorized data is exposed, or total reviewed human effort does not decrease.
Human authority
Licensed producer or designated agency reviewer decides whether a submission is complete and suitable to send; carrier decides underwriting and acceptance.

[bls] [applied-epic] [repo-surveyjs]

Policy-service request triage and document preparation

Classify incoming service requests, locate policy context and draft a checklist or response for review; do not alter policy terms or issue an endorsement automatically.

Measure
Net human minutes per correctly routed and producer-approved request, including exceptions and reopenings
Reject the idea if
Reject if a request is routed to the wrong client/policy, stale information is sent, or an external write occurs without approval.
Human authority
Agency service owner and licensed producer retain authority over advice, policy changes and carrier communication.

[applied-epic] [ams360] [repo-activepieces]

Certificate-of-insurance request handling

Gather request facts and prepare a draft from verified policy records; require an authorized reviewer before release and never infer coverage beyond the policy.

Measure
Human minutes per accurate, authorized certificate, with corrections, exceptions and recipient verification counted
Reject the idea if
Reject if a certificate states unsupported coverage, goes to an unauthorized recipient, or review/reissue effort offsets the time reduction.
Human authority
Authorized agency staff verifies policy facts and recipient before release; the certificate does not amend policy or create carrier authority.

[ams360] [repo-surveyjs] [repo-activepieces]

Renewal preparation and exception queue

Surface upcoming expirations, missing renewal information and changed fields for producer review; do not select limits, recommend replacement coverage or bind renewal.

Measure
Human minutes per on-time, producer-approved renewal packet; missed deadlines, exceptions and rework
Reject the idea if
Reject if stale or incomplete records suppress a renewal exception, a recommendation is presented as fact, or producer review does not reduce net effort.
Human authority
Licensed producer owns coverage comparison, recommendation and renewal/bind decision; carrier owns underwriting and issuance.

Business value and a falsifiable pilot

No agency baseline, invoices, achieved automation rate, review/rework measurement, operating cost, realized cash savings or eligible-population measurement was verified. Null is not zero.

Within one authorized independent agency, compare matched redacted or synthetic submissions, service requests, certificates and renewals under the incumbent and supervised candidate workflows. Pre-register authority boundaries and rollback conditions before live data.

  • Human effort including review and rework is not lower than the matched baseline.
  • Any policy, client or carrier data crosses an unauthorized boundary or an unapproved external write occurs.
  • A draft, reminder or extracted field is treated as a coverage decision, bind, claim decision or carrier acceptance.
  • Measured first-year net cash is not positive for a cash-savings offer; capacity gains alone are not cash.
Read the shared value model

Limits

  • This is not insurance, legal, underwriting, claims or financial advice, product admission or an industry-wide value forecast.
  • Independent-agency software adoption, prices, carrier acceptance and realized savings were not verified; named offerings are not assumed installed.
  • Carrier-versus-agency authority, producer licensing, state rules, privacy/security, accessibility and retention need agency-specific review.
  • No generic repository is treated as a replacement for an agency-management system, policy system of record or carrier portal.
  • Applied Systems agency-management URL returned HTTP 521 on the independent parent recheck; do not infer fresh capability or availability from that failure.
