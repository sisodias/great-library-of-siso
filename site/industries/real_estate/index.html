US real-estate brokerages

Start with administrative listing and transaction coordination: capture supplied facts, route requests, maintain checklists and prepare drafts for review. Follow Up Boss and Propertybase demonstrate that incumbents already advertise lead, listing and deal workflow capabilities; any pilot must measure against the actual brokerage stack and retain licensed-professional authority.

What the evidence supports

DOJ describes the Fair Housing Act as prohibiting discrimination in housing transactions, while CFPB Regulation X governs real-estate settlement-service conduct. Those authorities support a narrow automation boundary around clerical collection, routing, reminders and draft preparation; they do not establish an automation rate or authorize software to decide advertising, access, steering, financing or settlement matters.

Processes to test

Listing intake, completeness check and broker-approved publication

Collect supplied listing facts and media, flag missing fields, maintain an approval checklist and prepare a draft listing for review; do not invent facts, set price, choose audience, make accessibility/fair-housing determinations or publish without authorization.

Measure
Human minutes per accurate, broker-approved published listing, including review, corrections and exceptions
Reject the idea if
Reject if unsupported or discriminatory content is published, a critical field is silently changed, or reviewed human effort does not decrease.
Human authority
Broker or designated authorized staff verifies facts, advertising and fair-housing compliance and approves publication; software does not decide audience, eligibility, steering or lawful treatment.

Lead intake, response routing and showing coordination

Capture inquiries, deduplicate supplied contact details, route by broker-defined rules, send approved scheduling options and track confirmations; do not qualify protected classes, steer, recommend a property, or make an eligibility/financing decision.

Measure
Net human minutes per correctly routed and confirmed showing, including exceptions, reschedules and review
Reject the idea if
Reject if routing or messaging uses protected-class proxies, sends to the wrong person/property, exposes data, or review/rework offsets the time reduction.
Human authority
Brokerage staff retain authority over client communication, property recommendations, access and any fair-housing compliance judgment.

Under-contract milestone, document and inspection-request coordination

Maintain a broker-configured checklist, request missing supplied documents, schedule reminders and prepare a review queue; do not interpret contracts, advise on inspection/financing, calculate settlement amounts or release funds.

Measure
Human minutes per on-time, correctly documented broker-approved milestone, including review, rework and exceptions
Reject the idea if
Reject if a deadline, obligation or settlement fact is misrepresented, an unauthorized external write occurs, or net reviewed effort is not lower.
Human authority
Broker, attorney, lender, settlement provider and other authorized parties retain contract, legal, lending, settlement and funds authority; the workflow only coordinates supplied facts and reminders.

[cfpb-respa] [propertybase] [repo-condo] [repo-espocrm]

Post-close handoff, service-request intake and referral follow-up

Log a client request, route it to the selected authorized provider, prepare a status checklist and record acknowledgement; do not recommend financial/legal products, give advice, alter a contract or make a provider/eligibility decision.

Measure
Net human minutes per correctly routed and acknowledged request, with review, exceptions and reopenings counted
Reject the idea if
Reject if a referral is represented as advice or endorsement, a request is misrouted, private data is exposed, or review/rework eliminates the net effort reduction.
Human authority
Client and the selected licensed or otherwise authorized provider decide advice, product, legal and financial matters; brokerage staff approve communications and retain supervision.

Business value and a falsifiable pilot

No brokerage baseline, invoices, achieved automation rate, reviewed/reworked effort, operating cost, realized cash saving or eligible-population measurement was verified. Null is not zero.

Within one supervised brokerage, compare matched redacted or synthetic listings, inquiries, showings, transaction milestones and service requests under the incumbent and candidate workflows. Pre-register authority, privacy, fair-housing and rollback conditions before live data.

  • Human effort including review and rework is not lower than the matched baseline.
  • Any protected or private data crosses an unauthorized boundary or an unapproved external write occurs.
  • A listing, route, reminder or extracted field is treated as legal, financial, fair-housing, financing, settlement or other professional advice/decision.
  • Measured first-year net cash is not positive for a cash-savings offer; capacity gains alone are not cash.
Read the shared value model

Limits

  • This is not real-estate, legal, financial, lending, settlement, fair-housing or professional advice, product admission or an industry-wide value forecast.
  • Brokerage software adoption, prices, MLS permissions, realized savings and outcome prevalence were not independently verified; named offerings are not assumed installed.
  • State licensing, broker supervision, fair-housing, privacy/security, accessibility, advertising, referral, disclosure, contract and records-retention requirements need local review.
  • No generic repository is treated as a replacement for an MLS, brokerage CRM, transaction-management, accounting, escrow, lender or settlement system of record.
