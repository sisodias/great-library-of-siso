US recruiting and staffing agencies

Start with supervised requisition intake, scheduling and evidence-preserving status coordination. Existing ATS/CRM remains the system of record until a pilot proves quality, privacy, permissioning and net effort reduction. Vendor capability and repository features do not establish adoption, fairness or savings.

What the evidence supports

O*NET describes human-resources-specialist tasks including recruiting, screening and interviewing applicants. NIST's AI Risk Management Framework and the EEOC's employment-AI materials support risk identification, documentation and human oversight; the EEOC page was inaccessible in this run and is not used as supporting evidence. No independently verified percentage of recruiting work automated is established.

Processes to test

Client requisition intake and completeness review

Normalize supplied role, location, schedule and authorization fields; flag missing or inconsistent inputs; do not approve a requisition, infer protected traits or invent requirements.

Measure
Net human minutes per complete, approved requisition, including exceptions and rework
Reject the idea if
Reject if material role constraints are silently altered, privacy-sensitive fields are collected without purpose, or total reviewed effort does not fall.
Human authority
Authorized account/recruiting lead approves role requirements, client commitments and lawful data collection.

[onet-hr] [greenhouse] [repo-surveyjs]

Candidate sourcing, screening coordination and interview scheduling

Deduplicate supplied records, route approved outreach drafts and offer available interview slots; do not rank, reject, infer protected characteristics or make an employment recommendation.

Measure
Net human minutes per correctly scheduled, consent-respecting interview; duplicate/misroute and accommodation exceptions
Reject the idea if
Reject if an unauthorized or stale message is sent, candidate availability/accommodation is mishandled, or an automated screen changes access to a human review.
Human authority
Recruiter and client hiring authority control sourcing criteria, screening, accommodation and selection decisions.

[onet-hr] [greenhouse] [workable] [repo-calcom]

Interview feedback collection and evidence-linked review

Collect structured feedback against approved rubrics, show missing fields and preserve provenance; do not convert text into a score or disposition without authorized review.

Measure
Reviewer minutes per complete feedback packet; correction rate and permission/privacy exceptions
Reject the idea if
Reject if ungrounded inferences, disparate error patterns, unauthorized access or missing reviewer acknowledgement survive the workflow.
Human authority
Named recruiter and hiring panel retain interpretation, disposition and employment-decision authority.

[nist-ai-rmf] [greenhouse] [repo-surveyjs]

Client/candidate status updates and placement follow-up

Draft status updates from current approved records, surface missing placement milestones and schedule reminders; require review before external sends and keep payroll/contract authority upstream.

Measure
Net human minutes per accurate approved update, including retries, opt-outs and exception review
Reject the idea if
Reject if stale or unauthorized status is sent, recipients cross candidate/client boundaries, duplicate reminders occur, or the process bypasses employment/contract authority.
Human authority
Recruiting/account lead controls recipient, content, candidate status, placement and client communication decisions.

Business value and a falsifiable pilot

No client baseline, measured trial, price, staffing cost or outcome evidence was supplied; released time is not counted as cash.

Run a time-boxed, read/draft-only pilot on a matched sample of requisitions and interviews with synthetic or consented records. Compare incumbent and pilot net human effort, review/rework, exceptions, permission/privacy incidents, accessibility accommodations and decision-authority checkpoints.

  • any unauthorized employment recommendation or disposition
  • critical privacy, permission or accommodation failure
  • no net effort reduction after review/rework
  • unsupported claim of cash savings or automation percentage
