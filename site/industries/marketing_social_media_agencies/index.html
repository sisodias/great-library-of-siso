US marketing and social-media agencies

Agencies can test supervised drafting, scheduling and reporting around an existing approval chain. Platform permissions, consent, disclosures, account ownership and human release authority remain gates; vendor feature pages do not establish adoption, quality or savings.

All industriesCode valuationFoundryProject / agent JSON

Research dated 2026-09-05. Business value remains unmeasured. Software descriptions are documented capabilities; each project must verify fit, rights and outcomes before adoption.

United States research pilot; client contracts, platform terms and applicable state/federal advertising rules require an authorized review.

What the evidence supports

O*NET describes marketing-manager work including planning programs, coordinating promotional activities and measuring campaign results. Platform publishing APIs document permission and media/account constraints, so automation should be measured as bounded task assistance rather than autonomous campaign authority.

Processes to test

Campaign brief to channel-specific content calendar

Normalize an approved brief into draft channel variants, dates and required assets; do not infer claims, audience permissions or final strategy.

Measure
Human minutes per approved calendar, including revisions and exceptions; rejected or unsupported claims per calendar.
Reject the idea if
Reject if total review/rework effort is not reduced or an unsupported claim, audience/consent mismatch or missed approval survives.
Human authority
Named client/agency approver owns strategy, claims, audience and release decision.

[onet-marketing-manager] [buffer-publish] [repo-activepieces]

Approved campaign asset and copy production

Prepare drafts from approved claims, brand rules and supplied assets; preserve provenance and require human accessibility, factual and brand review.

Measure
Human minutes per approved asset/package, including review and rework; critical factual, rights or accessibility defects.
Reject the idea if
Reject if review does not reduce net effort or a factual, rights, disclosure or accessibility defect reaches client approval.
Human authority
Client/agency approver owns claims, rights, disclosure and final creative acceptance.

[onet-marketing-manager] [buffer-publish]

Cross-platform scheduling and publication

Queue only explicitly approved content to authorized accounts; surface platform errors and never bypass permissions, consent or release gates.

Measure
Human minutes per successfully approved and published post, including exception handling; unauthorized or failed publication count.
Reject the idea if
Reject if unauthorized/incorrect-account publication occurs, exceptions erase time reduction or platform permission/consent evidence is incomplete.
Human authority
Named account owner or agency approver authorizes each release; platform account authority remains external.

[meta-content-publishing] [tiktok-content-posting] [buffer-publish] [repo-activepieces]

Campaign delivery and performance reporting

Collect available delivery metrics, reconcile missing data and draft a traceable report; do not claim causality or optimization without human interpretation and baseline context.

Measure
Human minutes per accepted report, including reconciliation and rework; missing/incorrect metric fields and unsupported causal statements.
Reject the idea if
Reject if report errors or reconciliation effort offset time saved, or a material metric/causal claim lacks provenance and review.
Human authority
Agency analyst and client approver own interpretation, recommendations and business decisions.

Business value and a falsifiable pilot

No client baseline, authorization, adoption, pricing or controlled pilot data was available; missing data is not zero.

Paired, approval-gated pilot on a bounded set of client-approved campaigns; compare incumbent workflow with the same brief and count preparation, review, rework, exceptions, publication correctness and reporting reconciliation.

  • net human effort is not reduced
  • unauthorized account or unapproved content is published
  • critical factual, rights, disclosure, privacy or accessibility condition fails
  • reporting errors or missing provenance are not caught before client delivery
Read the shared value model

Limits

  • No client data, account tokens, live posts or campaign results were accessed.
  • Vendor and project documentation establish advertised capability, not adoption, prevalence, quality or savings.
  • Platform API availability, permissions, review requirements and terms vary by account, region and product.
  • FTC pages were inaccessible during retrieval; no FTC claim is used as supporting evidence.
  • No prices, staffing rates, conversion lift or cash savings are asserted.
