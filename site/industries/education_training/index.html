US adult vocational and continuing-education providers

Start with supervised learner intake, cohort logistics, attendance/progress evidence and completion-report preparation. Keep the provider's learning, student and reporting systems authoritative until a pilot proves record accuracy, privacy, accessibility and lower reviewed effort. No adoption, price, savings or outcome forecast is used.

All industriesCode valuationFoundryProject / agent JSON

Research dated 2026-09-05. Business value remains unmeasured. Software descriptions are documented capabilities; each project must verify fit, rights and outcomes before adoption.

United States research pilot; provider, funder, state workforce and program-specific reporting requirements require authorized review.

What the evidence supports

US Department of Labor guidance describes workforce and apprenticeship programs with participant, training and completion/reporting responsibilities. That grounds an inventory of intake, scheduling, attendance and evidence-preservation work, but does not establish an automation rate; eligibility, admissions, credential and licensing decisions remain authorized human work.

Processes to test

Adult learner or employer-sponsored training intake and record completeness review

Normalize supplied contact, cohort, prerequisite and consent fields; detect missing or duplicate records; do not decide admission, eligibility, accommodation, funding or licensing status.

Measure
Net human minutes per complete, authorized intake record including exceptions and rework
Reject the idea if
Reject if unauthorized fields are collected, records are merged incorrectly, protected information crosses a permission boundary, or total reviewed effort is not reduced.
Human authority
Provider-designated admissions or program staff control intake policy, eligibility, accommodations, consent and any funding decision.

[dol-wioa] [canvas] [frappe-learning-docs] [repo-frappe-lms]

Cohort, instructor and live-session logistics coordination

Draft schedules, reminders and approved roster changes from current records; flag conflicts; do not assign a learner to a regulated pathway or promise a seat, accommodation or credential.

Measure
Net human minutes per accurate, approved session change; roster, duplicate-send and missed-session exceptions
Reject the idea if
Reject if a stale or wrong-recipient reminder is sent, a roster change is unauthorized, accessibility or accommodation needs are lost, or review/rework offsets the effort reduction.
Human authority
Program and instructor owners approve schedules, rosters, accommodations, cancellations and learner communications.

[dol-apprenticeship] [canvas] [bigbluebutton-docs] [repo-bigbluebutton] [repo-activepieces]

Attendance and learner-progress evidence collection and exception review

Collect attendance or activity events and surface missing, conflicting or late evidence with provenance; do not infer mastery, make a grade or treat a connection event as completion.

Measure
Review minutes per accepted evidence packet; missing/incorrect event rate and unresolved exceptions
Reject the idea if
Reject if attendance is falsely asserted, evidence provenance is lost, critical exceptions rise, or a system event is used as a grading, completion or credential decision without authorized review.
Human authority
Instructor or designated program staff validate attendance/progress evidence and retain grading, completion and accommodation authority.

[dol-wioa] [canvas] [bigbluebutton-docs] [repo-bigbluebutton] [repo-frappe-lms]

Completion and workforce-program report preparation

Assemble approved attendance, progress and completion fields into a reviewable report with source links; do not award a credential, determine eligibility, certify a licensure requirement or submit without authorized sign-off.

Measure
Net human minutes per accurate, approved report; correction, rejection, late and unauthorized-submission rate
Reject the idea if
Reject if unsupported completion or eligibility appears in a report, an unauthorized submission occurs, material corrections increase, or review/rework removes the effort reduction.
Human authority
Authorized provider/funder reporting owner approves completion, eligibility, credential and submission decisions.

Business value and a falsifiable pilot

No provider baseline, achieved automation rate, review/rework data, operating cost, avoided spend or eligible-population measurement. Null is not zero.

Within one authorized provider, compare matched redacted or synthetic cohorts and reporting periods under the incumbent and supervised candidate workflows; pre-register permitted fields, approval points and retention rules.

  • Human time including review/rework is not lower than matched baseline.
  • Any learner record crosses an unauthorized boundary or an unapproved external send/submission occurs.
  • Attendance, progress, completion, eligibility, credential or licensing authority is inferred from an automation event without authorized review.
  • Measured first-year net cash is not positive for a cash-savings offer; capacity gains alone are not cash.
Read the shared value model

Limits

  • This is not admissions, credentialing, licensing, education, legal or workforce-program advice, product admission or an industry-wide forecast.
  • Child data is out of scope; adult-provider systems may still contain sensitive learner information requiring data minimization and access controls.
  • Current software adoption prevalence, prices, learner outcomes, accessibility and security were not verified; named offerings are not assumed installed.
  • No generic repository is claimed to replace a provider's student, learning, reporting or professional-authority systems; rights, privacy, retention, accessibility, restoration and jurisdiction-specific requirements remain product-owner gates.
