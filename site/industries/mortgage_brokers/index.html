US mortgage brokerages

The narrowest useful pilot is supervised mortgage-file administration: collect borrower materials, identify missing items, track disclosure/signature status and route lender conditions. Floify and LendingPad advertise incumbent loan-origination workflow capability; any measured value must include review, rework, exceptions, privacy and regulatory controls.

United States mortgage-broker research pilot; state licensing, privacy, fair-lending and lender-specific requirements require broker review.

What the evidence supports

CFPB Regulation Z requires specified mortgage disclosures and timing, while Regulation B governs notice when a creditor takes adverse action. These rules support measuring administrative completeness, routing and acknowledgement work; they do not authorize software to decide creditworthiness, eligibility, pricing or adverse action.

Processes to test

Borrower inquiry and application-package intake

Collect supplied facts, create a checklist and flag missing or inconsistent documents; do not assess creditworthiness, eligibility, affordability, pricing or approval.

Measure
Human minutes per complete, broker-reviewed application package, including exceptions and rework
Reject the idea if
Reject if a missing or conflicting fact is silently accepted, protected data is exposed, or reviewed human effort does not decrease.
Human authority
Licensed broker or designated processor verifies facts and package completeness; creditor and authorized underwriters retain credit and approval authority.

Income, asset and identity document collection and exception queue

Receive, classify and route supplied documents; flag unreadable, stale or missing items for human review; do not verify eligibility or infer borrower income for a lending decision.

Measure
Human minutes per correctly classified and broker-reviewed document set, with exceptions, rework and privacy incidents counted
Reject the idea if
Reject if documents are misrouted, stale or unreadable items are treated as accepted, unauthorized access occurs, or review/rework offsets the reduction.
Human authority
Broker processor and lender underwriter verify documentation; software does not determine sufficiency for credit.

[floify] [lendingpad] [repo-activepieces]

Disclosure delivery, acknowledgement and signature-status follow-up

Prepare approved packets, record delivery/signature status and send reminders; do not alter disclosures, assert legal receipt, or bypass timing, identity or consent requirements.

Measure
Human minutes per accurately delivered and broker-acknowledged packet, including review, exceptions and reissue effort
Reject the idea if
Reject if a packet is changed without approval, sent to the wrong party, recorded as received without evidence, or required timing/consent controls fail.
Human authority
Broker and creditor compliance staff control disclosure content, timing, identity, receipt and legal sufficiency.

[cfpb-reg-z] [floify] [repo-documenso]

Lender-condition routing and closing-readiness follow-up

Track lender requests, route borrower responses and surface aging or missing conditions; do not waive conditions, clear underwriting, set terms or declare a loan ready to close.

Measure
Human minutes per correctly routed and authorized condition response, including exceptions, escalations and rework
Reject the idea if
Reject if a condition is incorrectly marked cleared, a deadline or exception is suppressed, an unauthorized lender write occurs, or net reviewed effort is not reduced.
Human authority
Authorized broker and lender underwriter decide whether conditions are satisfied and whether a loan may proceed or close.

Business value and a falsifiable pilot

No broker baseline, invoices, achieved automation rate, review/rework measurement, operating cost, realized cash savings or eligible-population measurement was verified. Null is not zero.

Within one authorized brokerage, compare matched synthetic or redacted application packages, document sets, disclosures and lender conditions under the incumbent and supervised candidate workflows. Pre-register authority, privacy and rollback conditions before live data.

  • Human effort including review and rework is not lower than the matched baseline.
  • Any borrower or lender data crosses an unauthorized boundary or an unapproved external write occurs.
  • An extracted field, reminder or draft is treated as a credit, eligibility, pricing, adverse-action, underwriting or approval decision.
  • Measured first-year net cash is not positive for a cash-savings offer; capacity gains alone are not cash.
Read the shared value model

Limits

  • This is not mortgage, legal, lending, underwriting, credit or financial advice, product admission or an industry-wide value forecast.
  • Broker software adoption, prices, lender acceptance and realized savings were not verified; named offerings are not assumed installed.
  • State licensing, fair lending, privacy/security, accessibility, e-signature, disclosure timing, retention and lender-specific controls require agency-specific review.
  • No generic repository is treated as a replacement for a licensed loan-origination system, lender portal, compliance system or human underwriting authority.
  • The BLS loan-officer task page was blocked during retrieval and is not used as supporting evidence; no task prevalence or automation rate is inferred from the block.
