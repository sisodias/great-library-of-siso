Decision to change

Which registry fields, Library projections, evidence interfaces, and action-return contracts should become the standard infrastructure for every SISO God Question?

Success criteria

A cold human or agent can find every standing question, its state, decision target, evidence gaps, falsifiers, watch triggers, and current answer lineage from one generated Library surface. · Existing repositories, books, videos, research, and operational receipts can attach to a question through owned evidence systems without being copied into the public registry. · A question can justify a bounded action candidate and later receive a verified learning return without the Library authorizing or executing the action. · At least one repeated mechanism discovered through a question can be evaluated and promoted into its independently owned capability Work.

Falsifiers

The design requires a second question registry or central operational database to remain coherent. · The visible dossier accumulates metadata but cannot improve a real research, routing, or investment decision. · Useful evidence cannot be connected without leaking private corpora, machine paths, client data, or operational topology into the public Library. · Maintaining the question contract costs more attention than the repeated research and decision errors it prevents.

Evidence gaps

No evaluated end-to-end user flow yet proves that the portfolio view helps a cold operator choose the next valuable investigation. · Claim, contradiction, assumption, experiment, mandate, observation, and learning-return objects do not yet share one proven cross-system receipt contract. · No three-question cohort yet measures authoring overhead, evidence reuse, decision impact, or capability promotion rate. · MEASURED 2026-08-04: extraction does not scale to the corpus. Claim extraction over the 932-book philosophy shelf alone needs ~400M tokens against a 150M budget (267%); the full 41.5M-passage corpus needs ~33,200M (22,133%). The budget covers ~187,500 passages. So evidence selection must be question-driven; 'extract everything and index it' is arithmetically impossible, not merely wasteful. · The retrieval unit is unresolved. Passages are the evidence substrate but not the search surface -- a paragraph is not a thing you can agree or disagree with. Claims (position + grounding quote + confidence) are ~2 orders of magnitude fewer and match the Evidence Engines ingest contract, but the extraction layer that produces them does not exist. · No standing agent operates the loop. Every piece of infrastructure built to date is supply; nothing continuously reads a question, finds bearing evidence, and updates an answer. This is the gap between a warehouse and an organ.

Watch triggers

A new God Question cannot express a decision target, falsifier, evidence gap, or refresh condition in the current contract. · Foundry or Knowledge ships a question-addressable export that can replace a manual evidence link. · The first approved experiment returns an Observation Receipt and exposes a missing Library lineage field. · Three heterogeneous questions complete a full research-to-decision loop and justify stabilizing or deleting provisional fields. · A standing agent proposes a change to the Library or Foundry and it survives independent review -- the first evidence that self-improvement is real rather than asserted.

Source scopes

the existing Frontier Question Works, metadata seeds, answer-lineage model, and question-driven research architecture · the generated Research index and individual Question Work reading surfaces · publication-safe Foundry, Knowledge, Evidence Engine, Event, ADR, Release, and Snapshot interfaces · privacy-safe receipts from real research, routing, repository, book, and implementation work

Answer shape

A versioned God Questions operating architecture with a generated Observatory, machine-readable question-program contract, evidence and action-return interfaces, ownership boundaries, acceptance fixtures, and explicit promotion and kill gates.

Refresh policy

Re-answer when a real question cannot use the contract, a source or action interface changes, three-question evaluation evidence lands, or measured operating overhead exceeds the value created.

QA-GQ009-CANONICAL-EXTENSION · QA · active · medium

The existing Work, Release, Snapshot, and Event spine can support question-program visibility without a second registry. QA-GQ009-METADATA-VALUE · QA · challenged · low

A compact assumption, evidence, and action-learning projection improves cold-reader reconstruction enough to justify its authoring overhead. Scope: question reconstruction economics · Falsifier: Cold readers or agents cannot reconstruct a decision path more accurately, or the repeated authoring cost exceeds the errors and rediscovery avoided. · Depends on: QA-GQ009-CANONICAL-EXTENSION · Evidence: EC-GQ009-CHECKPOINT, EC-GQ009-ESTATE-RECONCILIATION · Review: 2026-08-02

QA-GQ009-OWNER-BOUNDARY · QA · challenged · low

Typed public references can connect question demand to returned learning without granting approval, execution, or evidence-adjudication authority to the Library. Scope: action and learning authority · Falsifier: A real action-to-learning chain cannot remain reconstructable unless the Library stores private payloads or represents a proposal as approval or observation as accepted truth. · Depends on: QA-GQ009-CANONICAL-EXTENSION · Evidence: EC-GQ009-JOINT-BRIDGE, EC-GQ009-PRIVACY-FAIL, EC-GQ009-ESTATE-RECONCILIATION · Review: 2026-08-02
