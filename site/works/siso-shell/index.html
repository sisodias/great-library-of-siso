Why it matters

The bank separates navigation, reusable parts and page-family compositions. A consumer selects a reviewed source revision and supplies its own public-safe data. Updates become deliberate upgrades: review the source, register its evidence, select it in a Snapshot, re-pin the consumer, and verify the rendered result. Template presence and an old example do not establish deployment, project ownership or acceptance.
