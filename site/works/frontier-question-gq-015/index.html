Decision to change

Choose the amount, form and destination of an important output using actual decision value and attention cost.

Success criteria

A reader can find the outcome, supporting evidence, uncertainty and decision they actually need to make. · Reuse the appropriate existing surface and measure attention saved against maintenance and notification cost.

Falsifiers

An output creates more navigation or notifications without improving understanding or decision quality. · A polished page obscures the needed action, source evidence or unresolved uncertainty.

Evidence gaps

Publishing more pages or sending fewer messages alone does not prove a better human interface.

Watch triggers

New owner-approved outcome or counterexample changes the decision evidence. · The relevant source, operating constraint or acceptance boundary materially changes.

Source scopes

Existing public project pages and decision contracts · Owner-approved observations of readers using an important output

Answer shape

A verified improvement to an existing decision surface with reader observations, limits and a reusable presentation lesson.

Refresh policy

Review when relevant owner evidence or decision conditions change; registration starts no collector, benchmark, scheduler or new execution lane.
