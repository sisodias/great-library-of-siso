Decision to change

Choose intake, deduplication, source interpretation and task-routing practices before any live integration or outward response.

Success criteria

Replay a bounded input set and reconcile proposed changes against the owner's source-read list. · Distinguish human intent, assistant proposals, delivery copies, later corrections and the correct project owner.

Falsifiers

The intake misses a later correction, duplicates a request or treats an assistant proposal as human authorization. · The resulting change is assigned to the wrong owner, loses its source or crosses an outward-action boundary.

Evidence gaps

A parsed message or task row does not prove complete media coverage, correct authorship or an accepted change.

Watch triggers

New owner-approved outcome or counterexample changes the decision evidence. · The relevant source, operating constraint or acceptance boundary materially changes.

Source scopes

Public intake and task-ownership contracts · Owner-authorized export or synthetic replay cases with private payloads retained by their owner

Answer shape

A replay receipt that joins source occurrences, interpretation, owner assignment and accepted/rejected changes without publishing private content.

Refresh policy

Review when relevant owner evidence or decision conditions change; registration starts no collector, benchmark, scheduler or new execution lane.
