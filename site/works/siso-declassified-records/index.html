source_review · 2026-08-07

Four safety tiers are defined (standard, enhanced_privacy, hazardous_technical, restricted_metadata_only) with the governing principles that a capability manual must not be assembled from individually public records, that official release does not eliminate risk, and that a name in an intelligence or law-enforcement file is not proof of guilt, affiliation, reliability or agency employment. The last of these directly constrains any future People Graph link built from this material.research/declassified-government-records/rights-safety-and-ethics.md

source_review · 2026-08-07

The department holds that FOIA release and declassification are not synonyms, separating formal declassification, FOIA, mandatory declassification review, systematic review, statutory special collections and never-classified archival-open material, and rules that the system must never silently move from source-asserted truth to research conclusion.research/declassified-government-records/catalog-contract.md
