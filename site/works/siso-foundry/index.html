The Library’s reading

Why it matters

Good software already contains years of hard-won domain knowledge. Foundry makes that work discoverable and tests how it could serve a real business need, so each project does not have to start its research again. The Library preserves the resulting identities and receipts; Knowledge owns durable retrieval, and the product owner still decides what to adopt.
