Decision to change

Choose which variations and whole-application patterns to curate for a real host, and what to leave project-specific.

Success criteria

Demonstrate a needed component or flow in the actual host with its interaction and accessibility requirements. · Include rights, integration, maintenance and removal costs in the comparison.

Falsifiers

A larger catalogue increases selection or integration work without improving the accepted application. · A curated component fails the host's required behavior, accessibility, rights or user acceptance.

Evidence gaps

Catalogue breadth and a good standalone preview do not prove coherent assembly or fit in another context.

Watch triggers

New owner-approved outcome or counterexample changes the decision evidence. · The relevant source, operating constraint or acceptance boundary materially changes.

Source scopes

Public component, repository and adoption contracts · Authorized host-integration examples and acceptance evidence

Answer shape

A compact, evidence-backed building set with supported contexts, rejected alternatives, integration examples and explicit gaps.

Refresh policy
